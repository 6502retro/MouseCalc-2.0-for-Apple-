    How to get files on and off your Apple II disks
    • Easiest: open your hard-drive or floppy image here directly. On the PC → Apple II side, choose the same image under step 3 and pick a folder; you get back an updated copy of the image to put in place of the old one (keep a backup).
    • One file at a time: CiderPress II can extract a Mouse Calc file (keep the #f80000 ending) or add the file from the .zip this page makes.
    • Mouse Calc sheets are up to 62 columns (A–BJ) by 254 rows. Mouse Calc has no text formulas, dates or VLOOKUP, so those cells arrive as their current values, and the page tells you which ones.
    • Some Mouse Calc templates refer to themselves (random numbers, population steps). In Excel these are circular references; the workbook turns on one-pass iteration so they behave the same way.
